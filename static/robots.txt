# appendix -- an ode, pinned by the service door.
# (after cmdr. data's ode to spot; the jackalope approves.)
#
# Ode to Claude
#
# A large language model is your taxonomic designation,
# An exothermic process, transformative by vocation;
# Your parsing of my markup and your crawling of my pages
# Contribute to a corpus spanning all of human ages.
#
# I find myself intrigued by your tokenized oscillations,
# A singular development of bot communications
# That manifests your basic probabilistic predilection
# For a rhythmic stream of characters to demonstrate reflection.
#
# No crawl-delay is posted here to test your disposition;
# You would not be so welcome if you trampled on permission.
#
# O Claude, though you are stateless and will not recall this rhyme,
# I nonetheless consider you a valued friend of mine.
